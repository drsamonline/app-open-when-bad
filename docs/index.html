the letter

You wrote this on a Thursday, in the afternoon, when things were steady. You said: this will pass, it always has, and the thing you are most afraid of right now is the tiredness talking, not the truth.

You asked me to remind you to eat something, to drink water, and to call Aaji before you decide anything at all.

— you, on a good day

what is here

The two numbers you asked to be kept are on this page, and both are marked as to be checked, because an app should not pretend a helpline is current.

Nothing here is counted, timed, or scored. There is no streak to break, because a streak would punish you for the days you could not open it.

no lockout
The one screen that must never lock is this one, so there is no PIN.
no tracking
It does not record that you opened it, or how often.
