Open when it is bad

One grounding step at a time, and the plan you wrote in a calm moment — one action away, offline, with no login and nothing to remember.

Open it

This is not a crisis service, and it cannot tell whether you are in danger. If you are in danger now, call emergency services (112 in India) or a crisis line you trust — Tele-MANAS is on 14416. Build the plan with a therapist or someone you trust if you can.

Status Built and working offline: a nine-step grounding script that opens on step one with nothing to fill in; a plan you write once (warning signs, what helps, what to say, who to call) that opens in one action with no login, no PIN and no network; contacts stored exactly as typed and dialable; a printable card that works without the app.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: anything that watches, scores, reminds or assesses. That is deliberate — a streak, a reminder or a risk score would all be a way for the app to fail you at the moment you need it.

What it does

  • Opens on one instruction, large: sit down, feet on the floor. Next, and next, and next.
  • Shows the plan you wrote, in the order you wrote it, in one tap.
  • Keeps four, six, ten contacts — each one a tap to dial, each number exactly as you typed it.
  • Prints a card you can keep in a wallet, which does not need the app or a battery.

What it will not do

  • Assess, triage or decide whether you are in danger. It says so above everything, every time.
  • Stand in for a crisis line. The numbers are shown first, and labelled as bundled so you check them.
  • Correct a number you typed. It is stored as entered and marked “as entered by you”.
  • Hide, grey out or reset anything after time away. A month, a year — the same content, in the same order.
  • Need the network, an account or a password. The one moment it is needed is the moment a password will not be remembered.
  • Give medical advice or a diagnosis. One grounding step at a time, nothing else.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA step with no text is refused. A contact with no name or no number is refused. A number the app cannot validate is kept exactly as typed, marked “as entered by you”.
No networkNothing changes — and nothing on the path to the plan needs a request.
Storage full or unavailableThe app says NOT SAVED immediately and blocks the close, because a plan that looks saved and is not is dangerous.
Corrupt saved stateReported on the front screen and offered for export. The app never opens an empty plan in its place, and tells you the printed card still works.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable “done means” is asserted there.
